## Featured Projects

### 1. HoloManipulate — Gesture-Controlled 3D Workspace

**Technologies:** Python · OpenCV · MediaPipe · Open3D · NumPy · PySide6

A computer-vision-based application designed to let users interact with 3D models through hand gestures instead of traditional mouse-driven controls.

**Key features and development goals**
- Real-time hand tracking through a webcam.
- Gesture recognition for model manipulation.
- Rotation, translation, and scaling through hand movements.
- 3D model loading and scene interaction.
- Modular separation of tracking, gesture recognition, model control, and rendering.
- CPU-friendly development without requiring a dedicated NVIDIA GPU.

**Repository:** [HoloManipulate](https://github.com/M-i-c-h-e-a-l-05/HoloManipulate)

### 2. Human Video Composition — Gesture-Controlled Video Interaction

**Technologies:** Python · OpenCV · MediaPipe · NumPy · TensorFlow Lite

A real-time video composition system that combines human segmentation with gesture-controlled video regions.

**Key features**
- Segments a person from a live webcam feed.
- Composites the segmented person over a video background.
- Uses hand gestures to select, move, and resize video regions.
- Supports interactive manipulation of cut-out video regions.
- Preserves the foreground person above the background and movable video layers.
- Uses a modular architecture for tracking, segmentation, gesture control, and compositing.

**Repository:** [Human Video Composition](https://github.com/M-i-c-h-e-a-l-05/Human_Video_Composition)

### 3. Person Segmentation & 2D-to-3D Human Reconstruction

**Technologies:** Python · OpenCV · MiDaS · ZoeDepth · NumPy

An experimental computer vision project exploring human-region extraction and the conversion of 2D visual information into 3D representations.

**Development focus**

- Combining segmentation masks with depth information.
- Capturing human views from multiple angles.
- Preparing cropped human regions for reconstruction workflows.
- Exploring 3D geometry generation and OBJ-based output.

**Repository:** [Person Segmentation](https://github.com/M-i-c-h-e-a-l-05/Person_Segmentation)

## How I Approach Engineering

I enjoy working across the complete development process rather than focusing exclusively on model training or application interfaces.

- **Research and experimentation:** Compare approaches and evaluate which methods are practical for a given problem.
- **Implementation:** Build working prototypes using appropriate libraries and frameworks.
- **Modular architecture:** Separate tracking, inference, business logic, rendering, and interaction where appropriate.
- **Hardware-aware development:** Consider memory, CPU performance, and available compute resources.
- **Integration:** Connect individual models and components into usable applications.
- **Continuous improvement:** Debug failures, refine controls, and iterate on existing implementations.

My projects are a mix of working implementations, ongoing development, and system concepts. I aim to document their actual capabilities and limitations clearly.
